* Give **highest priority to reviews that explicitly mention “wishlist” or discuss wishlist-related behaviour/features**.

* Exclude reviews that contain excessive emojis, meaningless characters, spam, or extremely low-information content.
* Remove duplicate or substantially repetitive reviews.
* Preserve the original review text, rating, review date, and other useful metadata where available.
* Do not fabricate, paraphrase, or generate reviews.

Prioritise discussions from approximately the **last 18 months** so that the research reflects the current Myntra product experience.
