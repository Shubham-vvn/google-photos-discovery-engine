Strategic Core Problem

Solving Photo Retrieval When Memory is Incomplete

Users accumulate thousands of visual memories in Google Photos. While exact keyword searches work, retrieval collapses when memory is episodic, vague, or associative (e.g. "that small café in Goa" or "the medicine pill strip from last year"). This AI engine uncovers why retrieval fails and drives non-monetary product solutions.
